Beyond painting: creating an experience

At Estudio Mattea, we don’t treat school murals as standard paint jobs. An educational space is a living environment that influences learning, pride, and community connection. We approach every project as a tailored visual rebranding experience designed specifically for indoor classrooms, corridors, and outdoor courtyards.

By blending deep storytelling with specialised artistic craftsmanship, we turn empty, tired walls into immersive spaces that celebrate your school’s unique culture, history, and educational values.

We don’t decorate walls — we help a school see its own identity, then paint it at scale.

Three insights from painting our first three schools

Transforming three diverse educational spaces taught us exactly what makes a school mural project run smoothly and land meaningfully.

Insight 01

Students protect what they help make

When pupils contribute drawings or keywords at the concept stage, the finished wall belongs to them. Involvement early on builds lasting pride — and dramatically reduces later damage.

Insight 02

The academic calendar is the real constraint

Schools run on fixed rhythms. Planning execution around terms, exams and breaks — not around our schedule — is half of what makes a project painless for facility managers.

Insight 03

One wall shifts a whole corridor

A single, well-placed mural changes how an entire shared space feels. You rarely need to paint everything; you need to paint the right surface.

The school transformation process

We make the entire journey effortless for facility directors and exciting for students. Here is how our end-to-end process works.

School identity discovery & staff / student mini-interviews

We start by listening. We hold a mini-interview session with school leaders, teachers, and students to understand your history, core values, and educational vision. Students share drawings or keyword ideas, giving them direct involvement in the creation of their space.

Free wall analysis & digital mockup preview

Send us photos and measurements of your targeted indoor or outdoor walls. We design a scaled 2D visual render placed directly over the photo of your space, so you can visualise the exact outcome before any paint touches the wall.

Material specification & flexible scheduling

We establish an execution plan tailored to your academic calendar. We can also prepare all surfaces using non-toxic, odourless, low-VOC paints where required.

On-site artistic execution

Our team carries out the work safely and cleanly — either during off-hours, or during school terms as an engaging live-art experience for the students.

Protective sealing & final handover

We apply a protective seal at the end, ensuring your mural stays vibrant and easy to clean for years to come.

Tailored applications: indoor vs. outdoor spaces

Every surface has different demands. We specify materials, prep and sealing around where the mural actually lives.

Indoor

Classrooms, corridors & libraries

  • Certified low-VOC, odourless paints for active learning spaces
  • Fast turnaround around a single break or weekend
  • Scrub-resistant finishes for high-touch walls and reading nooks
Outdoor

Courtyards, facades & sports zones

  • Weather- and UV-resistant systems built for years outside
  • Anti-graffiti sealing so the wall stays cleanable
  • Large-format work staged safely around student movement
